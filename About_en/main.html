About Me

  1. Home
  2. About Me

Wang, Peir-Ru

I am the Founder of Quantum Perspectives Co., Ltd., dedicated to advancing the industrialization and global development of quantum technology. I am also a Researcher at 𝒊𝑁𝑆𝐼𝐺𝐻𝑇 𝒊ℏ, promoting quantum education and knowledge sharing. Through these two platforms, I aim to bridge theory and application, fostering global dialogue within the quantum community.

I previously worked as a postdoctoral researcher at National Tsing Hua University, focusing on low-temperature STM studies of two-dimensional quantum materials such as TaS₂, NbS₂, and VS₂. I earned my Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering at the same university, where I proposed the Superconducting Critical Coupling Constant and studied mechanisms for enhancing superconductivity.

My expertise spans mechanical engineering, physics, materials science, and quantum technology. My research centers on superconductivity and scanning tunneling microscopy (STM), integrating theory and experiment to explore quantum structures. In engineering practice, I hold U.S. and Taiwan patents for continuously variable transmissions (CVT). During my Ph.D., I also developed the automated resistivity–temperature system (EA-1 Veronica) and oxide powder mixer (EA-2 Veronica), demonstrating strong integration of control theory and experimental automation.
